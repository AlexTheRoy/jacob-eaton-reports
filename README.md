# Jacob Eaton Agency reports

Static client reporting site for GOAL account #953. Edition 11-12-26. Reporting period: since launch, May 1 through Nov 12, 2026.

No build step. Open `index.html`, or deploy the folder as is (see `vercel.json`: clean URLs, security headers, noindex).

## Campaign Reports
- Campaign Overview (Home Kansas, Home Nebraska, KS Auto, Auto (CA) and a separate Marketplace row)
- Home Kansas Campaign Performance
- Home Nebraska Campaign Performance
- KS Auto Campaign Performance
- Monthly Sales by Source & Aid
- Source & ZIP Sales (with ZIP CSV)
- Lead-to-Sale Gap Analysis
- Inbound Call Review

## Website & Research
- Kansas & Nebraska Demographic Research
- Kansas & Nebraska Severe Weather Risk
- Kansas & Nebraska ZIP Data (CSV)

All conversion metrics are calculated on leads, not clicks. CPA is the headline metric, with cost per item alongside it. The status files carry no producer column, so there is no Producer Pipeline report. Premium is blank in every status file, so no premium figure is shown.
